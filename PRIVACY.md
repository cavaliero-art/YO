# YO and your privacy

YO has no account, no cloud, no ads and no tracking. What you put in YO stays on your PC.

## What stays on your PC
- Your habits, tasks, notes, reminders and Focus history.
- Your prayer city, settings, and what you mark as prayed.
- Your looks, settings, and what you typed in YO's bar.

All of it is in `%APPDATA%\YO` and in YO's own storage on your PC. YO never uploads it anywhere.

## What leaves your PC
- **Translate:** only the text you choose to translate is sent to MyMemory (translated.net), a free
  translation service, to be translated. Nothing else goes with it: no account, no name, no other data.
- **Updates:** a few times a day, the installed YO asks GitHub (github.com/cavaliero-art/YO) whether there
  is a newer version, and downloads it from there. GitHub sees that a computer asked, like any download.

Nothing else is sent. YO has no analytics and no crash reporting.

## What YO reads on your PC, and never sends
- The song that's playing (Windows media info), so YO can bop along and name it.
- Your installed apps, so "open …" can find them (kept in `apps.json`, rebuilt by itself).
- Files or text you drop on YO, only to show their name and path or count the words.

## Your control
In YO, open the tray menu, then **About YO, backup and privacy**, then **Your data**, to:
- **Back up** everything to one file, and **restore** it on this PC or another one.
- Keep a **weekly backup** by itself (in `Documents\YO backups`, the last 4 are kept). You can turn it off.
- **Delete all your data** from this PC. Your backup files stay until you delete them yourself.

Uninstalling YO keeps your data (so reinstalling brings it back). To remove it too, use
**Delete all my data** first, or delete the `%APPDATA%\YO` folder.

## Tunisia
YO doesn't collect or process anyone's personal data on a server, in line with Tunisia's personal data
protection law (Organic Law 2004-63). Everything stays with you.
